# Ethics, Scope, and Data Protection

These datasets are small snapshots of **public marketplace listings**, published for
schema review and prototyping. They are not a license to collect or republish personal data
at scale.

## Principles

1. **Public data only.** No login-only pages, private messages, account dashboards, or
   paywalled content.
2. **No contact data.** Public samples omit seller names, phone numbers, emails, and exact
   coordinates. Do not add them in pull requests.
3. **No circumvention of authentication.** Do not use these schemas to bypass logins,
   paywalls, or access controls.
4. **Respect the platform.** Read and follow each marketplace's terms of service,
   robots directives, and rate limits. A successful request is not automatically permitted.
5. **Data minimization.** Collect only the fields you need. If a field is not used, do not
   publish it.
6. **Purpose limitation.** Do not use this data for spam, harassment, stalking, automated
   unsolicited outreach, or discrimination.
7. **Indonesian law.** Personal data handling must respect Indonesia's UU PDP and other
   applicable rules. When in doubt, remove the field.

## What is intentionally excluded

- Seller names and identifiers
- Phone numbers and email addresses
- Exact latitude/longitude
- Private or login-gated content
- Full seller-written descriptions in the job-board sample

## Reporting a concern

If you believe a sample contains personal data that should not be public, open an issue
with the dataset name and field, or contact the maintainer through GitHub. Removal requests
are handled before feature requests.
