Design rules

Stable marketplace IDsTitles and prices change; the marketplace ID is the deduplication key.
Numeric + display pricesAggregate on price, QA against priceDisplay.
Null, not zeroMissing salary or mileage stays null — never a fake sentinel.

Recurring collection

The samples are free. For scheduled collection, these Apify Actors produce the same schemas: OLX, 4 job boards, Carsome. They are pay-per-result; the data and schemas here are not.
